# 🧠 AI Engineering — Awesome References

> Building applications on top of foundation models: LLMs, generative AI,
> retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agents, and prompt engineering. AI
> engineering focuses on **composing and orchestrating** models rather than
> training them from scratch.

## 🗺️ Roadmaps & curricula

- [AI Engineer Roadmap (roadmap.sh)](https://roadmap.sh/ai-engineer) — interactive AI engineer skill roadmap.
- [mlabonne/llm-course](https://github.com/mlabonne/llm-course) — a complete, hands-on course on LLMs (fundamentals, fine-tuning, deployment).
- [microsoft/generative-ai-for-beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/generative-ai-for-beginners) — 21-lesson beginner course on building with generative AI.
- [microsoft/ai-agents-for-beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/ai-agents-for-beginners) — beginner course focused on building AI agents.

## ⭐ Awesome lists & curated collections

- [aishwaryanr/awesome-generative-ai-guide](https://github.com/aishwaryanr/awesome-generative-ai-guide) — one-stop repo with roadmaps, research updates, courses, and interview prep.
- [Hannibal046/Awesome-LLM](https://github.com/Hannibal046/Awesome-LLM) — curated papers, models, tools, and frameworks for large language models.
- [Shubhamsaboo/awesome-llm-apps](https://github.com/Shubhamsaboo/awesome-llm-apps) — collection of LLM apps with RAG, agents, and multi-agent examples.

## 🔧 Learn by building

- [rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch](https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch) — implement a GPT-style LLM step by step in PyTorch.
- [DataTalksClub/llm-zoomcamp](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/llm-zoomcamp) — free bootcamp on building production LLM/RAG applications.
- [openai/openai-cookbook](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook) — practical examples and recipes for building with LLM APIs.
- [dair-ai/Prompt-Engineering-Guide](https://github.com/dair-ai/Prompt-Engineering-Guide) — comprehensive guide to prompt engineering techniques.

## 🧰 Core frameworks

- [langchain-ai/langchain](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langchain) — framework for composable LLM applications and agents.
- [run-llama/llama_index](https://github.com/run-llama/llama_index) — data framework for RAG over your own data.
- [langgenius/dify](https://github.com/langgenius/dify) — open-source platform for building and operating generative AI apps.
- [Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT) — pioneering project for autonomous, goal-driven LLM agents.
