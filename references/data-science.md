# Data Science — Awesome References

> Extracting knowledge from data: statistics, exploratory analysis, modelling,
> experimentation, and communicating results. Data science sits between
> analytics and machine learning.

## Roadmaps & curricula

- [AI & Data Scientist Roadmap (roadmap.sh)](https://roadmap.sh/ai-data-scientist) — interactive skill roadmap.
- [ossu/data-science](https://github.com/ossu/data-science) — complete free, self-taught university curriculum.
- [microsoft/Data-Science-For-Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/Data-Science-For-Beginners) — Microsoft's 10-week beginner curriculum.
- [virgili0/Virgilio](https://github.com/virgili0/Virgilio) — structured "mentor" study paths from statistics to ML.

## Awesome lists & curated collections

- [academic/awesome-datascience](https://github.com/academic/awesome-datascience) — a huge curated list of courses, tutorials, projects, and tools.
- [r0f1/datascience](https://github.com/r0f1/datascience) — a curated list of Python data science packages and tools.
- [khuyentran1401/Data-science](https://github.com/khuyentran1401/Data-science) — curated cool data science tools, tips, and end-to-end project examples.

## Hands-on notebooks & references

- [jakevdp/PythonDataScienceHandbook](https://github.com/jakevdp/PythonDataScienceHandbook) — the full handbook as runnable Jupyter notebooks.
- [donnemartin/data-science-ipython-notebooks](https://github.com/donnemartin/data-science-ipython-notebooks) — practical notebooks for pandas, scikit-learn, statistics, and deep learning.
- [labmlai/annotated_deep_learning_paper_implementations](https://github.com/labmlai/annotated_deep_learning_paper_implementations) — annotated, runnable implementations of key papers.

## Core libraries

- [pandas-dev/pandas](https://github.com/pandas-dev/pandas) — data manipulation and analysis.
- [numpy/numpy](https://github.com/numpy/numpy) — numerical computing foundation.
- [scikit-learn/scikit-learn](https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn) — classical machine learning in Python.
- [statsmodels/statsmodels](https://github.com/statsmodels/statsmodels) — statistical modelling and tests.
